## 9. Assumptions & decisions

- Source: M5 (Walmart) from the public Nixtla mirror, https://github.com/Nixtla/m5-forecasts/raw/main/datasets/m5.zip. Only a small raw extract is kept in `data/raw/m5_subset/` and is never modified.
- Filtering: department FOODS_3, stores CA_1, TX_1, WI_1; history 2014-05-22 -> 2016-05-22.
- Sampling: items must pass the zero-share rule (<= 20% zero days in every store over 365 days) and the launch rule (a sale within the first 28 days in every store); then the top 50 by units, ranked only on data up to 2016-02-28 (no look-ahead). Result: 41 items, 123 series.
- Christmas (Dec 25): stores are closed; 246 rows imputed with the same weekday one week earlier and flagged `is_imputed`.
- Stockouts: zero-sales runs of 7+ days are flagged `likely_stockout` (2,125 days) and not imputed; sales are censored demand, so sales != true demand.
- Outliers: units > Q3 + 3 x IQR per series are flagged `is_outlier` (218 days) and kept.
- Missing sell prices are forward- then back-filled within each series.
